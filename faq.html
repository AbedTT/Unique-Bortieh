Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, packages and process.

Our team includes Chartered Accountants (ICAG), Chartered Tax Practitioners (CITG), degree holders in procurement, supply chain management and economics, HND holders in Purchasing and Supply, and specialists in other fields. Together they cover statutory audit, financial reporting, tax compliance, procurement and store operations.

We focus on schools and educational institutions, churches and religious organizations, hotels and hospitality businesses, and small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in Accra and the Greater Accra Region.

We offer three packages. Starter covers bookkeeping, payroll and management accounts. Business Control adds annual financial statements, risk assessment and internal control. Complete Business Support adds forensic and internal auditing spot-checks, accounting software installation, staff training and ongoing support.

Fees depend on the scale of your operations, transaction volume, number of employees and the package you select. Agreed fees are set out in a formal engagement letter. Contact us for a proposal tailored to your organization.

Yes. Our services can be provided on a monthly, quarterly, annual or project basis, depending on the requirements of your organization.

Our risk and control review evaluates operational and financial risks, cash management controls and internal fraud-prevention mechanisms. Forensic safeguards include targeted reviews of cash handling, inventory management and authorization levels, and our Complete package adds periodic spot-checks and unannounced cash and inventory audits.

We handle corporate income tax, Withholding Tax (WHT) and Value Added Tax (VAT), and provide tax planning, GRA audit representation, tax dispute resolution and tax compliance health checks. We also train your staff in tax awareness.

Yes. We deploy, set up and maintain QuickBooks (Online and Desktop), Tally Prime and local payroll systems. Our Complete package also covers chart of accounts setup and configuration of Sage and Xero, on-site staff training and ongoing technical support.

Our data handling protocols conform to the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). All personnel sign strict Non-Disclosure Agreements, and physical files are kept under lock and dual-key security at our office.

We begin with an introductory meeting and a review of your financial records, then diagnose your existing systems and recommend the best-fit package. Once we sign an engagement letter covering scope, service levels, confidentiality and fees, we onboard your team and provide monthly management accounts, quarterly reviews and annual statements.
