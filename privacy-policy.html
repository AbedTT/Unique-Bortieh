Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Unique-Bortieh Financial Consult ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, who else handles it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

2. Information We Collect

This website is a simple information site. The only information we collect through it is what you choose to send us:

  • Enquiry Form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), the service or package you are interested in, and your message.
  • Direct Contact: any details you give us when you email, call or meet us.

We do not ask for payment card details or passwords through this website, and we do not use analytics or advertising tools to track how you use it. Some technical information (such as your IP address and browser details) is received automatically by the outside services that deliver the website to you. These services are listed in Section 4.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and follow up with you
  • To prepare proposals and, if you become a client, to provide our services
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To detect and prevent spam, fraud and misuse of the website

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Outside Services Used by This Website

We rely on the following services to run this website. When you use the website, they receive the information described below, and they have their own privacy policies.

  • Web3Forms (web3forms.com): when you send our enquiry form, Web3Forms receives what you typed (name, email address, phone number, service interest and message) and delivers it to our email inbox.
  • hCaptcha (hcaptcha.com): the enquiry form uses hCaptcha to check that the sender is a person and not a robot. It loads only when you reach the enquiry form, and it receives your IP address, browser details and how you interact with the check.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com): supplies the website's typefaces. Google receives your IP address and browser details whenever a page loads.
  • unpkg (unpkg.com): supplies the website's icon set. unpkg and its content delivery network receive your IP address and browser details whenever a page loads.
  • Web Hosting Provider: the company that hosts this website may log IP addresses and request details as part of its normal operation.

These providers may process information outside Ghana. If you prefer not to use the enquiry form, you can contact us directly by email or phone using the details in Section 12.

5. Disclosure of Your Information

We share your information only in the following situations:

  • Service Providers: the outside services in Section 4, which we use to operate this website and receive your enquiries
  • Legal Requirements: when required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • With Your Consent: when you explicitly authorize us to share your information

6. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not set cookies of its own, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The outside services in Section 4 receive technical information such as your IP address when a page loads, and hCaptcha may use cookies or similar technologies to tell people from robots. You can control how your browser handles cookies and stored data through its settings.

7. Data Security and Retention

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, and our personnel are bound by confidentiality agreements. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We keep enquiry details only for as long as we need them to respond to you, to provide our services if you become a client, and to meet our legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct inaccurate data, to have your data deleted where we no longer need it, or to object to how we use it. We will respond as required by the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). You also have the right to complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will take steps to delete the information promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
