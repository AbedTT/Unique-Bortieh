Risk Assessment & Internal Control

Find the weak points in your finances and close them before they cost you.

Employee theft, petty cash loss, payroll fraud and internal control gaps quietly drain profits and erode stakeholder trust. We help you find where you are exposed and put practical controls in place.

What This Service Includes

  • Risk and control review: A comprehensive evaluation of your operational and financial risks.
  • Cash management controls: Controls over how cash is received, held and paid out.
  • Fraud prevention: Internal mechanisms that prevent fraud and theft before they happen.
  • Internal control frameworks: Our Chartered Accountants establish the internal control frameworks that fit your organization.
  • Financial procedures: Financial procedures for the way your organization handles money.
  • Inventory and expenditure controls: Controls over stock and spending, especially important in hospitality.
  • Control enforcement training: Staff training so that the controls are actually followed.
