Who We Are

Built on Trust.

Unique-Bortieh Financial Consult is a professional consultancy firm based in Accra, Ghana. We specialize in financial accounting, taxation, financial management, risk assessment, internal control systems, training and accounting software support.

We give schools, churches, hotels and growing businesses senior-level financial leadership without the overhead of a full-time, multi-tiered finance department — on a monthly, quarterly, annual or project basis.

Service Packages

Flexible Packages for
Every Stage of Growth

Three professional packages, from basic accounting support to a complete financial management and accounting technology solution — available on a monthly, quarterly, annual or project basis.

Package 1

Starter

Designed for small businesses, startups, small schools and growing enterprises requiring reliable basic bookkeeping and financial compliance.

  • Bookkeeping: daily sales and expenditure records, ledgers and monthly bank reconciliation.
  • Payroll: monthly salary computation, payslips, PAYE and Tier 1/2 SSNIT deductions and filings.
  • Management Accounts: monthly income and expense summaries and budget variance reporting.
Package 2

Business Control

Designed for established SMEs, churches, medium schools and hospitality businesses seeking tight internal financial oversight and asset protection.

  • Everything in Starter, plus full annual financial statement preparation.
  • Risk & Control Review: evaluation of operational and financial risks, cash management controls and fraud-prevention mechanisms.
  • Forensic Safeguards: targeted reviews of cash handling, inventory and authorization levels to detect and prevent employee theft.
Package 3

Complete Business Support

Our turnkey solution for organizations requiring an integrated financial, forensic, software and human capacity solution.

  • Everything in Business Control.
  • Software Integration: installation, chart of accounts setup and configuration of QuickBooks, Sage, Xero or Tally.
  • Staff Training: on-site training on software operation, basic bookkeeping and internal control adherence.
  • Ongoing Audit Support: periodic spot-checks, unannounced cash and inventory audits, and ongoing technical support.
